● NISAR (NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar) glacier velocity data is processed
  through the ITS_LIVE (Interagency Time Series of Land Ice Velocity and
  Elevation) system. Here's how to access it:

  Direct Access

  1. ITS_LIVE Portal: Visit https://its-live.jpl.nasa.gov/ — this is the
  primary, login-free interface for accessing all velocity data, including
  NISAR-derived estimates once available.
  2. Data Discovery: Search by glacier region or download velocity maps directly
  from the portal.

  NISAR Data Processing Pipeline

  - NISAR scenes are ingested by the Alaska Satellite Facility (ASF) DAAC
  immediately after acquisition
  - Velocity estimates are typically available within 2 hours of scene
  cataloguing
  - Feature tracking is performed on 20,000+ image pairs daily using InSAR
  techniques

  Real-Time Notifications

  Set up near-real-time alerts for new NISAR scenes:
  - Use NASA's Common Metadata Repository (CMR) to subscribe to NISAR collection
  notifications
  - Subscribe via AWS SQS to receive messages when new scenes are ingested

  Alternative Data Sources

  For historical glacier velocity data (pre-NISAR), explore:
  - MEaSUREs Greenland Ice Velocity datasets
  (https://nsidc.org/data/nsidc-0481/versions/4) at NSIDC
  - ITS_LIVE regional velocity products (https://nsidc.org/sites/default/files/d
  ocuments/user-guide/nsidc-0776-v002-userguide.pdf)

  The velocity estimates come from InSAR processing, which measures glacier
  surface displacement between radar acquisitions, providing sub-meter accuracy.

  Sources:
  - ITS_LIVE global glacier velocity data in near-real time
  (https://tc.copernicus.org/articles/19/3517/2025/tc-19-3517-2025.pdf)
  - Alaska Satellite Facility Distributed Active Archive Center
  (https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/centers/asf-daac)
  - MEaSUREs Greenland Ice Velocity datasets
  (https://nsidc.org/data/nsidc-0481/versions/4)
