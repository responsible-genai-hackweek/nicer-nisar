● Based on the NISAR Data User Guide, here's how to approach getting glacier
  velocity estimates from NISAR:

  Key Data Products for Glacier Velocity

  InSAR-based Interferogram Products (GUNW/RIFG): NISAR generates unwrapped
  interferograms and geocoded unwrapped products that are the foundation for
  velocity estimation. These capture phase differences between radar
  observations separated in time, which can be converted to displacement
  measurements.

  Processing Workflow:
  1. Use Level 2 products (geocoded) like GUNW (Geocoded Unwrapped
     Interferogram) or GOFF (Geocoded Offset) products — these are
     analysis-ready and georeferenced, making them suitable for glacier velocity
     work
  2. Leverage ISCE3 — NISAR's open-source InSAR Scientific Computing Environment
     is designed for SAR/InSAR processing and can be used for custom velocity
     derivation workflows
  3. Offset tracking — GOFF products measure ground displacement between
     acquisitions, directly providing velocity information

  Recommended Approach

  - Start with GUNW or GOFF Level 2 products rather than complex raw data
  - Use ISCE3 or compatible tools (GDAL, rasterio, Python/NumPy workflows) to
    process the data
  - Consult the NISAR tutorials section of the guide for specific code examples
  - Contact ASF User Services (uso@asf.alaska.edu) or the Earthdata Forum for
    mission-specific guidance on ice sheet/glacier applications

  The dual-frequency capability (S- and L-band) also provides advantages for
  penetrating through snow/ice and improving coherence for velocity estimation.
