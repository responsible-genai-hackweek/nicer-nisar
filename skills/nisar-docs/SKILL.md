# nisar-docs

The NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar (NISAR) is an Earth observation satellite (EOS) equipped with dual-frequency (S- and L-band) synthetic-aperture radar (SAR) jointly developed by NASA and ISRO, launched by ISRO on 30 July 2025.

The most comprehensive source of information on how to use and access NISAR data is the NISAR Data User Guide at <https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/>.
